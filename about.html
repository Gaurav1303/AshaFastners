About us

Ten years of

Asha Fasteners has supplied nuts, bolts and washers out of Millerganj, Ludhiana since 2015 — first to the metal framing trade, now to solar, railways, automobile, HVAC and electrical work across India.

Our story

Built around one promise:
the part is on the shelf.

The firm started in 2015 as a proprietorship with a single aim — give the metal framing industry a complete fastener solution instead of sending buyers to four different suppliers for one assembly.

That shaped how we hold stock. When we list a size range, every size in it is on the shelf, because a bracket that arrives without its channel nut is a bracket nobody can fit. Mixed-size orders leave together.

The range grew as customers pulled it along. Solar mounting brought in the flange and serrated ranges. Rolling stock brought high tensile grades and test certificates. HVAC brought threaded rod, long nuts and drop-in anchors. Today it runs from M3 up to M100 across roughly fifty product lines.

What has not changed is who answers the phone. Ask for a pitch, a DIN number or a coating thickness, and the answer comes from someone who handles the stock.
